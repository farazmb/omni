Why Are Modern Smartphone Videos So Huge?

A 1-minute 4K video recorded on an iPhone or modern Android phone can easily consume 350MB to 500MB of storage. When you try to attach it to an email (25MB limit) or upload it to Discord without Nitro (25MB limit), you are blocked.

The Three Secrets to High-Efficiency Video Compression:

  • Resolution Downscaling: Downscaling a 4K or 1080p recording to crisp 720p reduces pixel count by over 50% without noticeable degradation on mobile screens.
  • Constant Rate Factor (CRF) Tuning: Adjusting bitrate dynamically so complex motion scenes get more bits while static scenes save massive data.
  • Audio Stream Optimization: Compressing unnecessary 5.1 surround audio into clean 128 kbps stereo AAC.
