Why Apple Uses HEIC & How to Overcome Compatibility Issues

Since iOS 11, Apple devices capture photos in High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format by default. HEIC cuts storage requirements in half compared to standard JPEGs while supporting 16-bit color depth. However, Windows PCs, Android phones, email clients, and many websites cannot natively display or open .heic files.

OmniConvert solves this instantly by decoding the HEIC data stream directly inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your photos never leave your device, ensuring maximum confidentiality for personal snapshots, business documents, and ID photos.

Key Advantages of OmniConvert HEIC Conversion:

  • Zero Server Uploads: Conversions happen entirely in your device's memory.
  • Preserve EXIF Metadata: Camera details, timestamps, and orientation are faithfully maintained.
  • Batch ZIP Export: Convert hundreds of iPhone photos at once and download a single organized ZIP.
